          Data & methodology

          CO—SAT is a parody. Satellites do not influence your life, except the ones that do (GPS, weather, your internet). Orbital elements © CelesTrak, used under their data policy: each dataset is fetched at most once every 3 hours, from a single caching server, with backoff on errors. All positions are computed on your device with SGP4 (satellite.js). Nothing about you leaves your browser.
